# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-4721 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Sales-record (inventory/sale/spoilage) logging, floor-staff/delivery
scheduling proposals, supply-order coordination, and food-safety-concern
flagging for specialized food retail stores (butcher, bakery, fishmonger,
greengrocer).

Out-of-scope: Finalizing food-safety-clearance decisions, overriding
allergen-exclusion requirements, direct refrigeration/slicing/processing-
equipment actuation, and food-safety-authority enforcement (health-department
clearance, inspection sign-off, license/permit actions).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
